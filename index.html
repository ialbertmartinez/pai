Who We Are

Precision Component Repair Backed by Experience

From our facility in Gilbert, Arizona, Perform Air International supports operators, MROs and distributors around the world. FAA, EASA and globally certified, we specialize in hydraulic, pneumatic, electro-mechanical, fuel, and water and waste systems for the commercial and military aviation industries.

What We Do

Component MRO Capabilities

Perform Air provides repair, overhaul and testing services across five primary aircraft component categories.

Every capability is searchable byPart NumberATA ChapterAircraft Type
ATA 29

Hydraulics

Repair, overhaul and testing of hydraulic components used throughout critical aircraft systems, including servo actuators, control valves, hydraulic pumps, accumulators, reservoirs, flight control components and other fluid power applications.

ATA 36

Pneumatics

Comprehensive support for pneumatic components including pressure regulating valves, shutoff valves, check valves, flow control valves, pressure sensors, and environmental and airflow system components.

ATA 28

Fuel

Repair, overhaul and testing of fuel system components including boost and transfer pumps, fuel valves, fuel de-icing valves and related mechanical assemblies.

ATA 24 & 27

Electro‑Mechanical

Specialized repair and overhaul of components combining electrical and mechanical systems, including AC and DC motors, motor-driven actuators, contactors, switches, blowers, cooling fans and related control assemblies.

ATA 38

Water & Waste

Repair and overhaul support for aircraft water and waste system components, including potable water tanks and valves, vacuum waste tanks, water heaters, flush valves, drain masts, pumps and related assemblies used throughout commercial aircraft.
